Key search terms from reading:
- PROTAC: proteolysis-targeting-chimera, which is the molecule type
- heterobifunctional: one end binds the target protein and the other binds an E3 ligase. they are joined by a linker
- E3 ligase: enzyme that tags target with ubiquitin for destruction. classic ones are CRBN and VHL
- molecular glue: achieves degradation through stabilising interaction between target protein and E3 ligase, no linker
- ternary complex: consists of the target, degrader and the ligase bound together, required for degradation

I noticed that PROTACs are structurally complex, so patent claim drafting is quite complicated
